SoftLedger — Project Description

SoftLedger is a lightweight business management and customer ledger application designed for small electronic-device charging businesses. It replaces physical notebooks used to record customer charging services, unpaid balances, and subsequent payments.

Main Points

Customer Management

Create and manage customer profiles.

Track each customer's complete transaction history.

Prevent deletion of customers with existing transaction history.


Charge & Payment Ledger

Separate charges and payments records.

Record every charging service and payment independently.

Support partial and multiple payments.


Automatic Customer Balance

Balance is computed from the ledger: Total Charges − Total Payments = Outstanding Balance

Avoids maintaining a potentially inconsistent stored balance.


Transaction Management

View individual charge/payment details.

Allow editing and deletion for correcting mistakes.

Preserve the underlying transaction history as the source of truth.


Business Dashboard

Provide an overview of outstanding customer balances, charges, payments, and business activity.

Designed to expand into reporting and analytics as the product develops.


Scalable Foundation

Start with the core notebook-replacement problem.

Gradually expand into broader business management features based on real store workflows.


Target Market

Small charging businesses and similar micro/small businesses that currently rely on physical notebooks to track customer debts and payments.



Core objective: turn a simple, error-prone physical customer ledger into a reliable digital system without imposing the complexity of traditional accounting software.
